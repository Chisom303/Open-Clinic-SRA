# Disclaimer

Open Clinic SRA is a free, community-maintained tool that helps small healthcare
practices structure a security risk analysis under the HIPAA Security Rule
(45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C).

- **It is not legal advice.** Nothing in this project, its questions, scoring, or
  reports is legal, compliance, or professional advice. Consult a qualified
  attorney or compliance professional for decisions about your organization.
- **It does not certify compliance.** Completing an assessment does not mean your
  organization is HIPAA compliant, and no score produced by this tool is a
  certification, attestation, or guarantee of any kind.
- **You are responsible for your risk analysis.** HIPAA requires covered entities
  and business associates to conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of risks
  to ePHI. This tool is an aid to that process, not a substitute for it.
- **Regulations change.** Content reflects the authors' understanding of the rule
  at the time each question was written. Check the `last_reviewed` date on each
  question and current guidance from HHS.
- **Do not enter patient information.** The tool asks about safeguards, not
  patients. Never type protected health information (PHI) into any field.
